Privacy policy

Last updated 12 August 2026. Written for a police-station pilot, not a consumer app.

Who we are

CrimeGPT is operated as a station-grade assistance system for the Kanad S.H.I.E.L.D. Ahmedabad City Police Innovation Challenge 2026 (PS-69EEFDFB90B99). It is not a public chatbot and it is not offered to the general public as a consumer service.

What is stored

  • Officer accounts: name, identifier, optional email and mobile, role, station, password hash, login timestamps, and lockout counters.
  • User preferences: interface language and later document-language choices.
  • Contact messages sent through this website, including IP and user agent.
  • Application settings, audit log rows, notifications, usage counters, and background job records.
  • In later steps: case facts, diary entries, and generated papers. Those records are not created in this foundation step.

Where it is stored

Data lives on the application server in a SQLite database under the instance directory and in local folders for uploads and generated files. Those folders are not published as static web assets. There is no consumer chatbot on a personal phone that retains station narratives.

Who can see it

Role-scoped officers at the bound station can see what their role permits once those modules exist. Super Admin can access platform-wide records and bypass station scope. Super Admin cannot impersonate another user’s session. Contact messages are visible to platform operators.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell narratives, case facts, or contact messages.
  • We do not use station content for advertising.
  • We do not put GEMINI_API_KEY or other secrets in the browser.

Cookies and session

A session cookie keeps you signed in. It is HTTP-only, SameSite Lax, and Secure in production. A language preference may be stored in the session for visitors who are not signed in. There is no third-party advertising cookie.

Retention

The configured retention window is {{ retention_days }} days for operational data unless a later lawful hold applies. Account deactivation can be requested through the contact form by a station administrator or the data subject’s authorised officer.

Contact

Privacy questions for this pilot go through the contact form. Emergency reports must not be sent here.
