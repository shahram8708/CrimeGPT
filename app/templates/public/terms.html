Terms of use

Last updated 12 August 2026. These terms apply to authorised officers and challenge evaluators.

Authorised users only

CrimeGPT is a station workspace. Accounts are provisioned by a station administrator or Super Admin. Self-registration is not offered in this step. You may use the system only if you are an authorised member of a participating unit or an evaluator given access for PS-69EEFDFB90B99.

Draft assistance, not a filing system

The product helps draft statutory police papers and a case diary. It does not file an FIR, does not write to CCTNS or ICJS, and does not replace a Magistrate or public prosecutor. AI-generated legal information may contain errors.

Officer remains responsible

You remain responsible for the statutory accuracy of every paper that leaves the station. Confirm BNS, BNSS, BSA provisions, gazette notifications, and court records before you rely on a suggestion. Do not treat a generated section list as a charging decision.

Acceptable use

  • Do not paste case facts into public consumer AI tools.
  • Do not share credentials or leave a signed-in session unattended in a public area of the station.
  • Do not scrape, probe, or attempt to bypass role or station scope.
  • Do not upload material you are not authorised to hold on a station system.

Access control

Super Admin may revoke access, lock an account, or change station bindings. Impersonation of another user’s session is forbidden, including by Super Admin.

Availability

This is a pilot. Service may be interrupted for maintenance or challenge operations. Do not use CrimeGPT as the only copy of a court-bound paper.
