How a remand is built

Four steps sit between a street fact and a paper the Magistrate can read. The officer stays in every step.

01

Capture facts

Names, place, time, exhibits, panchas, and medical notes go into one case pool - not into seven separate drafts.

02

Confirm sections

Suggested BNS / BNSS / BSA provisions are listed. The officer accepts, rejects, or edits before anything is printed.

03

Generate papers

The seven statutory forms pull from the same pool. Letterhead, station, and diary references stay consistent.

04

Keep the diary

Each material step is dated. When the file moves, the diary moves with it.
